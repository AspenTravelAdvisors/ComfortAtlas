    Data & methodology

    Tax is the published top statutory personal income tax rate for residents. The OECD series uses general government where applicable, while supplemental jurisdiction summaries may measure national rates only. Rates exclude individual reliefs and special regimes. Crypto gains, staking, tax residency and citizenship can change the analysis.

    Crypto is a July 2026 FATF snapshot of virtual asset service provider rules. Categories reflect reported prohibition and licensing or registration legislation. Other or undecided includes policies under development. Coverage varies by jurisdiction and rules can change.
